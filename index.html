Instructions for recruits (if this was shared with you)
  1. Save or bookmark this file — it's yours now, your own personal Beast Mode.
  2. Every time you show up, set the session date first — it decides whether it's a bench week or shoulder press week.
  3. Tap "Watch ↗" on any move you don't recognize before you attempt it with weight on it.
  4. Log what you lifted, tick it off, hit save. Future you will thank present you.
  5. Check the History tab occasionally to admire your own gains trajectory.
  6. Your logged data lives only in this browser, on this device — it doesn't sync back to whoever sent you this, and it doesn't need a Claude account or subscription. If you clear Safari's data or switch browsers, your log resets, so use the CSV export on the History tab as a backup.
  7. Beast Mode is self-administered. Consult reality (or a physio) if anything feels wrong.
